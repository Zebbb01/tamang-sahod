## Pag-IBIG maximum fund salary from February 2024 | DBM Circular Letter 2024-2, item 2.0

Under Pag-IBIG Fund Circular No. 460, the maximum fund salary to be used in computing the employee and employer savings was increased from P5,000 to P10,000 per month.

## Pag-IBIG contribution rates for employee and employer | DBM Circular Letter 2024-2, item 2.0

Section C of said Pag-IBIG Circular (Monthly Membership Savings) further specifies that, unless otherwise noted, the contribution rate of all Pag-IBIG I members, both mandatory and voluntary, shall be as follows:
Fund salary P1,500 and below - employee 1.0% - employer 2.0%
Fund salary over P1,500 - employee 2.0% - employer 2.0%
